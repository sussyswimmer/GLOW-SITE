Sleep is not wasted time. When you sleep, your body rests, and your brain keeps working.

During the day, you learn many new things. You read, you listen, and you talk with people. At night, your brain sorts this information. It keeps the important things and puts them in your long-term memory. This is why a good night of sleep helps you remember what you studied.

When you are tired, it is harder to pay attention. It is also harder to learn new words. Many experts say that most adults need about seven to nine hours of sleep each night. Children and teenagers usually need more.

Here are some simple tips for better sleep:

- Go to bed at the same time every night.
- Turn off your phone before you sleep.
- Keep your room quiet and dark.

If you study in the evening, try to finish early. A short review before bed, then a full night of sleep, is a good way to remember new words.
